# Product Boundary Summary

SC-EVM is designed exclusively as a developer preview for session-isolated context-control middleware.

## Out of Scope
- **Production Readiness:** SC-EVM is not certified for production workloads.
- **Enterprise Grade:** No enterprise features (e.g. multi-tenant authorization, encryption-at-rest) are provided.
- **Broad Multi-Tenant Deployments:** Public deployments are explicitly not supported.
