# RFC-XXXX: Title

- Status: Draft, Under Review, Accepted, Rejected, Superseded, or Withdrawn.
- Author: Person accountable for the proposal.
- Created: Date created in `YYYY-MM-DD` format.
- Reviewers: Required product, architecture, security, operational, or commercial reviewers.
- Supersedes: Earlier RFC numbers replaced by this proposal, or `None`.
- Superseded by: Later accepted RFC number, or `None`.

## Summary

State the proposed decision and intended outcome concisely.

## Motivation

Describe the problem, why it matters now, and why existing behavior is insufficient.

## Relationship to the Manifesto

Evaluate the proposal explicitly against all four decision questions:

- **Relevance:** Does it improve the relevance of context?
- **Isolation:** Does it strengthen isolation or preserve its guarantees?
- **Control:** Does it increase operator control or make policy more explicit?
- **Evidence:** Can its value be demonstrated with evidence?

Identify any manifesto principle or product pillar that the proposal constrains or risks.

## Relationship to the Product Boundary

Name every affected boundary classification, MVP commitment, non-goal, and commercial-visibility decision. State whether any classification would be superseded.

## Current State

Describe only verified implemented behavior, current limitations, and relevant evidence.

## Proposed Decision

Define the decision, boundaries, responsibilities, and behavior precisely enough for independent implementation and review.

## Alternatives Considered

List credible alternatives, including retaining the current state, and explain why each was not selected.

## Security and Privacy Impact

Describe changes to trust boundaries, data exposure, retention, deletion, credentials, isolation, and abuse cases. State `No material impact` with justification when applicable.

## Operational Impact

Describe deployment, reliability, observability, failure modes, support burden, and ongoing operating cost.

## Compatibility and Migration

Describe API, state, configuration, and deployment compatibility plus any migration or coexistence plan.

## Validation Plan

Define tests, measurements, acceptance thresholds, comparison controls, and evidence required before the decision is considered complete.

## Commercial-Claim Impact

State which external claims become supported, remain unsupported, or must change. Do not infer outcomes from implementation alone.

## Risks

List technical, product, security, operational, and organizational risks with mitigations.

## Rollback Plan

Define how to reverse the change without violating compatibility, isolation, retention, or customer commitments.

## Decision

Reviewers record the final outcome, conditions, date, and rationale here. Leave undecided while Draft or Under Review.

## Evidence

Link to source files, tests, evaluation artifacts, review notes, and other reproducible support for the decision.
