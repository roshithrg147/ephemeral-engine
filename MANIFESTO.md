# SC-EVM Product Manifesto

> **Governance:** This is SC-EVM's highest-level product philosophy. Changes require executive or founder-level review. Architecture, feature, and commercial documents must conform to it. See the [Product Boundary](PRODUCT_BOUNDARY.md) and [RFC process](rfcs/README.md).

## Vision

We envision a future in which intelligent software can work with people over long periods without losing what matters, confusing one context with another, or becoming less trustworthy as its history grows. Continuity should strengthen an intelligent system, not burden it.

## Mission

SC-EVM makes sustained AI interaction dependable by controlling what context is retained, what is relevant now, what remains separate, and what must be forgotten. Every day, we turn accumulated history into deliberate working context so that intelligent applications can remain useful, understandable, and governable over time.

## The Problem

Long-running AI systems accumulate more information than they can use well. As history grows, relevant facts compete with repetition, stale assumptions, unrelated topics, and instructions that no longer apply. Passing everything forward increases cost and operational burden without guaranteeing better judgment; cutting history blindly breaks continuity. The result is degraded relevance, unpredictable reasoning, and a growing web of corrective logic around a system whose behavior becomes harder to explain and control.

## Our Belief

**We believe intelligent systems become dependable not by remembering everything, but by carrying forward the right context under explicit control.**

## The Four Questions

These questions are SC-EVM's internal decision filter; every meaningful product or architecture decision should improve at least one:

1. Does it improve relevance?
2. Does it strengthen isolation?
3. Does it increase operator control?
4. Can its value be demonstrated with evidence?

## Product Definition

**SC-EVM is a session-isolated context-control layer that keeps multi-turn AI applications grounded in relevant memory while bounding the conversation history sent to their reasoning models.**

## Core Principles

### 1. Relevance Over Accumulation

More history is not the same as more understanding. SC-EVM preserves continuity by selecting what matters to the present task and refusing to treat every past interaction as equally useful. Context must earn its place.

### 2. Boundaries Create Trust

Every session has a clear identity, lifecycle, and end. Information must not drift across those boundaries by accident, and deletion must be a real product behavior rather than a vague intention. Trust begins with knowing where context belongs and when it is gone.

### 3. Control Must Be Explicit

Critical behavior should follow visible rules that teams can inspect, test, and govern. Intelligent judgment may remain probabilistic, but the policies that admit context, separate sessions, retain state, and permit actions must not be hidden behind unexplained behavior.

### 4. Evidence Governs Claims

We say only what observation can support. A compelling mechanism is not proof of a customer outcome, and a successful demonstration is not proof of dependable operation. Product confidence must be earned through repeatable evidence and honest limits.

### 5. Independence Preserves Choice

SC-EVM must remain useful as the intelligence beneath it changes. Customers should be free to choose how reasoning is supplied, where the system runs, and which application surrounds it. The product belongs at the context boundary, not inside someone else's ecosystem.

### 6. Simplicity at the Boundary

The system may contain deep engineering, but its contract with the customer must remain clear: provide the current intent, receive relevant continuity, and retain control over state. Complexity is justified only when it strengthens that contract.

## Product Pillars

### Relevance

SC-EVM determines what prior knowledge deserves to influence the present. Everything that interprets intent, recalls history, grounds reasoning, or rejects distraction belongs to this pillar.

### Isolation

SC-EVM keeps contexts separate, intact, and bounded throughout their lives. Everything that protects state, governs retention, restores continuity, or ensures deletion belongs to this pillar.

### Control

SC-EVM gives applications and operators clear authority over behavior. Everything that exposes the product, applies policy, manages actions, supports operation, or connects people to the system belongs to this pillar.

### Evidence

SC-EVM must be observable, testable, and accountable. Everything that measures behavior, records outcomes, validates assumptions, or reveals failure belongs to this pillar.

## Company Moat

SC-EVM's long-term advantage is not a single mechanism. It can be built through disciplined knowledge of how context fails, a growing body of representative evaluations, credible evidence about which policies work under which conditions, an architecture that keeps those policies enforceable, and operational expertise earned from real deployments. Each customer problem should deepen our understanding; each finding should improve our evaluation discipline; each improvement should be tested against prior evidence. The moat exists only if this learning compounds faster and more reliably than the underlying components become commonplace.

## Business Flywheel

1. Customers bring sustained, consequential interactions with real context failures.
2. Those interactions reveal where relevance, isolation, control, or continuity breaks down.
3. We convert those lessons into durable evaluation cases and clearer operating knowledge.
4. That evidence sharpens product policy and strengthens dependable behavior.
5. Better behavior earns broader use in more demanding environments.
6. Broader use produces richer lessons, returning the company to the first step with greater knowledge and stronger proof.

## What We Refuse To Become

- **An intelligence vendor.** We do not compete to create the underlying reasoning capability; we make sustained use of intelligence dependable.

- **A conversational destination.** We do not define ourselves by a chat screen. SC-EVM belongs inside many products and workflows.

- **A universal automation suite.** We do not collect unrelated actions simply because intelligent systems can perform them. Capabilities must strengthen the context-control mission.

- **An archive of everything.** Unlimited retention is not continuity. We preserve what serves the present purpose and respect the right to forget.

- **A hidden decision-maker.** We do not replace explicit product policy with behavior that teams cannot inspect, govern, or challenge.

- **A proprietary island.** We do not make customer choice an obstacle to adoption. SC-EVM must connect cleanly to changing applications and forms of intelligence.

- **A collection of clever mechanisms.** Technical novelty has no standing unless it advances relevance, isolation, control, or evidence.

- **A company that markets assumptions as facts.** We do not turn plausible ideas into claims before they have earned support.

- **A system that confuses complexity with value.** We will not ask customers to carry internal complexity that SC-EVM should absorb on their behalf.

## Product Litmus Test

A feature belongs in SC-EVM only when it strengthens the product's ability to deliver dependable context. Features primarily concerned with user interfaces, generic automation, content generation, workflow orchestration, productivity tooling, or unrelated integrations remain outside the core product unless they directly reinforce dependable context.

## Long-Term Ambition

If SC-EVM succeeds completely, dependable context becomes a standard layer of intelligent software rather than an improvised feature inside every application. Teams stop treating accumulated history as understanding and begin designing continuity with explicit boundaries, evidence, and control. Intelligent systems can then work alongside people for longer, across more consequential tasks, without trust declining as the relationship grows.
